Last updated: 26 September 2026

This policy explains how Orange Luna L.L.C-FZ ("Orange Luna", "we", "us") handles personal information in connection with our website at orangeluna.com and our services. We keep data collection to a minimum and we do not sell personal information.

Information we collect

This website does not use advertising trackers, and we do not run third-party marketing cookies.

How we use it

  • To respond to enquiries and provide the services you ask for.

  • To meet legal and record-keeping obligations.

Sharing

We share personal information only with service providers who help us run our business (for example website hosting and email), and only as needed. We may disclose information where required by law. We do not sell your personal data.

Retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected, or as required by law, and then delete or anonymise it.

Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you. To make a request, email hello@orangeluna.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "last updated" date above.

Contact
